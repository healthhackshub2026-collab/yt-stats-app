yt-stats

A private analytics tool for one creator's own YouTube channels.

yt-stats reads YouTube Analytics for the channels its owner has signed in with, once a day, and turns the numbers into a short report: views, watch time, subscribers, audience countries, retention and revenue. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.

Access is read-only. yt-stats cannot upload, edit, delete or comment on anything on YouTube.
