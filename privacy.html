yt-stats privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who uses this app

yt-stats is a private tool used only by its owner to review the performance of the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google OAuth, yt-stats reads:

  • YouTube Analytics reports for the signed-in channels (views, watch time, subscribers, countries, traffic sources, retention and, for monetized channels, estimated revenue) - scopes yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly;
  • basic channel and video information (titles, publish dates, video length, privacy status) - scope youtube.readonly.

Access is read-only. The app never uploads, edits, deletes or comments on anything on YouTube.

How data is stored and used

Daily summaries and the sign-in refresh tokens are stored in the owner's own Amazon Web Services account, encrypted at rest, and kept for up to 180 days. The data is used only to produce reports for the owner. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles of YouTube viewers. No personal data about viewers is collected; YouTube Analytics provides only aggregated statistics.

Google API Services User Data Policy

yt-stats' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted by deleting the app's database in AWS.
